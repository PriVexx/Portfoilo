About Me

MY JOURNEY

I'm Priyanshu Sharma, a first-semester B.Tech CSE student at JECRC University, Jaipur. I'm at the very beginning of a long, exciting road in computer science — and I'm absolutely fine with that.

Right now, I'm focused on building a solid foundation in programming through C and C++. At the same time, I'm exploring the AI landscape — experimenting with large language models, generative tools, prompt engineering techniques, and AI APIs.

I believe the best way to learn is to build things, break things, and ask a lot of questions. Every project I start — even the unfinished ones — teaches me something real that no textbook can replicate.

I'm drawn to where technology meets creativity — AI-generated images, autonomous agents, and the question of how these tools will reshape how we build, create, and think. I want to be someone who doesn't just use these technologies, but understands them deeply.
